What to do now
    Prototype scanner: this build estimates infection from colour and spot-pattern analysis of the photo itself, done in your browser. Swap in a trained CNN (e.g. a VGG16 / Faster R-CNN classifier served over an API) behind the "Analyze photo" button for production-grade accuracy.

    How the scan works

    Step 1

    Capture

    Take a clear, well-lit photo of the affected leaf, stem, or fruit — fill the frame, avoid heavy shadow.

    Step 2

    Analyze

    The scanner reads discoloration, spotting, and surface texture to match against known chilli disease patterns.

    Step 3

    Act

    You get a plain-language diagnosis, how it spreads, and the immediate steps to protect the rest of the crop.
